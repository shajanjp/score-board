About Score Board

Score Board is a simple, elegant way to track scores for your favorite card games, board games, or any friendly competition.

Designed with a focus on speed and aesthetic, it features a glassmorphism UI, real-time leaderboard updates, and persistent history.

Built with HTML, CSS (Tailwind), and Vanilla JavaScript. All data is stored locally in your browser.
